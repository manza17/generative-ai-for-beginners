# My dream AI startup

## Problem
A friend of mine owns a barbershop. He told me that managing appointments is one of the biggest problems he deals with. Today, many small businesses have this problem. For example, barbershops have to answer messages about availability on WhatsApp, check their calendar and offer some available time slots. While they are working, lots of messages pile up and this is a real hassle, because they don't reply on time and can lose customers. The usual solution is to hire a receptionist, but this is expensive and hard to afford for small businesses.
## How I would use AI
Since he told me that, I have been thinking about a solution: an AI assistant that answers on WhatsApp, where customers already write to ask for an appointment. This assistant can understand the customer's request written in natural language, for example "I want to book for Friday afternoon". Then, the assistant checks the real calendar through a REST API and confirms the booking, so it never makes up time slots. Each business should set up its own rules, such as opening hours and cancellation policy. This way, the assistant can answer questions about these rules without making up information.
When a customer confirms an appointment, the assistant saves it in a database. If another customer has already taken it, the assistant tells the customer and offers another time slot. This way, two customers can never book the same time slot.
## Impact
Before, customers had to wait for an answer and a confirmation from the business. With this solution, any customer can book an appointment at any time and get an instant reply. Besides, the business doesn't have to hire a receptionist, so the owner saves money and can focus on his work without needing to answer customers' messages.
